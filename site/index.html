    Try

    1. Arrows under PLAYER and LEVEL-LIST scroll the players and the 111 levels.
    2. Arrows beside RANKINGS scroll the ranking table.
    3. new player asks for a name on the message line. Type, then Enter.
    4. skip level, delete player and statistics answer on the message line.
    5. gfx-tutor, controls and the chip beside the hall of fame open their screens.
    6. OK starts the level: the game panel.
